Your words stay yours

No account. No ads. No analytics. No Open Flow server. Here is the whole story.

  • History stays on this phone unless you share it.
  • INTERNET is declared, but unused until you pick a cloud path or a model download.
  • Accessibility finds your text field and inserts what you said. Nothing else.
  • The mic only listens after you tap the bubble.
  • The phone’s speech engine may still use Google. That is your phone, not our server.
  • We hide on many bank apps. Their warning is theirs.
Want audio to never leave the phone? Turn on Whisper on phone in Settings. Otherwise the default path follows your device recognizer settings.
