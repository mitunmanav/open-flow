Vs other voice apps

Open Flow is not the most accurate listener. It keeps your keyboard, and it keeps history, dictionary, and snippets on this phone. MIT. No account.

If you need audio to stay on the phone, pick FUTO, Sayboard, or Whisper IME — not Open Flow. We use the phone’s speech engine. That is handy. It is not a local Whisper stack.
