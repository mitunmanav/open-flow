Privacy

Last updated 18 August 2026. Questions: GitHub Discussions. Bugs: GitHub Issues. Do not send personal data. No email.

  • No account. No ads. No analytics. No Open Flow server.
  • INTERNET is declared; unused until you pick a cloud path or a model download.
  • History stays on this phone unless you share it.
  • Accessibility finds a text field and inserts what you just said. Nothing else.
  • Microphone only while you dictate after you tap the bubble.
  • The phone’s speech engine may still use Google. That is the phone, not an Open Flow server.
  • We hide on many bank apps. Their warning is theirs.
