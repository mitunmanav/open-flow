Talk. Fix. Send.

Your keyboard stays. The bubble does the talking. Your words live in History.

You doWhat happens
Tap the bubbleListening starts
SpeakWords appear as you talk, if that setting is on
Tap again, or DoneStops, cleans up, inserts
Hold the bubbleTalks while held. Release inserts
XThrows the words away
Say “press enter” lastInserts, then sends
DragParks it anywhere. Stays up with the keyboard

Say it to fix it

“Strike that” erases the last sentence. “New paragraph” breaks the line. “Capitalize that” fixes the case. Numbers and lists format themselves.

Words you always get wrong

Dictionary swaps one word for another. Snippets paste a whole block when you say an exact trigger, like an address or email.

Find old dictations

Open the app → History. Copy, share, or delete there. Settings: keep everything, wipe after 24 hours, or never save.

Bubble gone?

  1. Normal text field? Not a password or bank app?
  2. Keyboard up?
  3. Accessibility still on? Force-stop turns it off.
  4. Mic allowed?
