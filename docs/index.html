A free Android app. A small bubble floats over the app you are already in. Tap, talk, tap again. Words go into that field.

Not a keyboard. MIT. No account. No ads. You pick the speech language in the app.

What this is for

We do not win on speech accuracy. This app keeps your keyboard and stores your words on this phone.

Always there

Bubble over your keyboard. You do not switch keyboards.

Your archive

History, dictionary, snippets — on this phone.

Your rules

Keep, wipe in 24 hours, or never save. No account.

Open source

MIT. No ads. INTERNET is declared; unused until you pick a cloud path.

Honest. INTERNET is declared. Open Flow does not use the network until you pick a cloud path or a model download. Speech still uses the phone’s system recognizer. On many phones that is Google, and audio can leave the device. Need “voice never leaves this phone”? Use a local Whisper or Vosk app — not this one. Compare.

Search, copy, share. Copy lives here — not on the bubble.

Cleanup + style

Rules on the phone. Not a cloud model.

How you use it

  1. Install the APK from Releases.
  2. Turn on Accessibility. Allow the mic.
  3. Open a normal text field (not a password or bank app).
  4. Tap the bubble → speak → tap again.
