Small app, honest pipes

Your keyboard stays. A bubble listens, tidies your words on the phone, and inserts them. That is the whole trick.

Where your words travel

The speech engine lives outside our app. INTERNET is declared — that is not the same as “audio never leaves.”

YouTap the bubble. Speak.
↓
Your appNotes, chat, browser — keyboard untouched.
↓
Open FlowBubble overlay. Inserts text only.
↓
Phone speechOn-device if the phone has it. Often Google. May leave the device.

One dictation, six steps

1 · TapStart listening
2 · SpeakPhone speech engine
3 · Tap againOr hold-to-talk, then release
4 · CleanupDictionary → snippet → rules → style
5 · InsertInto the focused field
6 · HistoryOn this phone, unless you chose never

X while listening throws the words away. The bubble stays up while the keyboard is up.
