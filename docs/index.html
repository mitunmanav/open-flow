Speak. It types.
Keep your keyboard.

Open Flow is a free Android app. A small bubble floats over other apps. Tap, talk, tap again. Words go into the field you were already in.

Not a keyboard. English only. MIT license. No account. No ads.

Honest: this app does not declare INTERNET and does not upload audio itself. Speech still uses the phone’s system recognizer. On many phones that is Google, and audio can leave the device. If you need “voice never leaves this phone,” use a local Whisper or Vosk app — not this one.

How you use it

  1. Install the APK from Releases.
  2. Turn on Accessibility (the bubble). Allow the mic.
  3. Open any normal text field.
  4. Tap the bubble → speak → tap again.

Copy old text from History in the app. There is no copy button on the bubble.

What it is not

  • Not Wispr Flow (that is closed and cloud).
  • Not an on-device Whisper model.
  • Not on F-Droid or Play yet.
  • Not a way around bank-app security warnings.
