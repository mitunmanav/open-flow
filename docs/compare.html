Flow vs built-in voice typing

Built-in dictation writes down every um and false start. Open Flow hands you the sentence you meant — and lets you keep your keyboard.

Say thisBuilt-in typesOpen Flow types
“let's meet at 5… actually 6pm”lets meet at 5 actually 6pmLet’s meet at 6pm.
“new paragraph … bullet points eggs milk bread”new paragraph bullet points eggs milk breadA new paragraph with a bulleted list: eggs, milk, bread.
“strike that”strike thatDeletes the last sentence.
The honest part: if your audio must never leave the phone, pick FUTO, Sayboard, or Whisper IME — not us. We use the phone’s speech engine by default, which on many phones is Google. Pick Whisper on phone in Settings for fully offline.
