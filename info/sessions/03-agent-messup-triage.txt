## How Wispr does it

**UX (Android)**  
- Floating bubble (not keyboard swap)  
- Keyboard stays  
- Tap bubble · speak · text lands in focused field  
- Same shell as Open Flow

**Core pipeline (not raw STT)**  
1. Capture speech  
2. **Cloud** ASR (always online · no offline)  
3. **AI cleanup layer** on the transcript  
4. Insert polished text  

**Self-correct (their #1 claim)**  
Say: *“Let’s meet at 4 pm, actually 3 pm”*  
Built-in STT writes all of it.  
Wispr → **“Let’s meet at 3 pm.”** only.  

Also:  
- “5… actually 6pm” → only **6pm**  
- Filler strip: um / uh / wait / false starts  
- Auto punct + lists  
- They call this **course correction** (model rewrite · not field append)

**Desktop pattern (reviews)**  
Hold hotkey → speak full thought → release → polish → paste.  
Not pure live-type like Gboard (some rivals do live stream; Wispr = **meaning cleanup** first).
