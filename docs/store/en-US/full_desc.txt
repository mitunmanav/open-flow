Open Flow is a Wispr-Flow style dictation bubble for Android. Tap the bubble, speak, and text lands in any app — you keep your keyboard.

• Floating bubble, not a keyboard replacement
• System speech or on-device Whisper
• History, dictionary, and snippets stay on this phone
• No account, no ads, no analytics

How it works: the bubble uses Accessibility only to find the focused text field and insert what you dictated. Password fields are skipped. History is local; cloud is opt-in only after you pick a brain or ear.

Privacy: INTERNET is declared but unused until you pick a cloud model or laptop URL. Default speech may use Google — that is the phone's engine, not our server. See Privacy policy for vendor list.
