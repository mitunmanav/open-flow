How to use it

  1. Install the APK. Turn on Accessibility. Allow the microphone.
  2. Open any regular text box — not a password or bank app.
  3. Tap the floating bubble, speak, and tap again. Words appear directly in that box.

What stays on your phone

Keyboard
We float on top. We do not replace Gboard or SwiftKey.
History
Search, copy, share, JSON export. All saved locally on your phone.
Voice Edits
“Strike that”, “new paragraph”, number formats. Rules execute on device.
Data Retention
Keep always, auto-clear in 24 hours, or never save. No account. MIT. No ads.
