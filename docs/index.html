How to use it

  1. Install the APK. Turn on Accessibility. Allow the microphone.
  2. Open any normal text box — not a password or bank app.
  3. Tap the bubble, speak, tap again. Words land right there.

asked a lot

Is the APK safe to sideload?

Yes. Code is MIT open source, built in public. Publisher is github.com/mitunmanav. Android warns on all sideloads — that is normal.

Why not on the Play Store yet?

Play review is slow and strict. Direct APK keeps us fast. Store listing is on the roadmap.

Does offline voice cost much?

Whisper is a single model download on Wi-Fi. Default mode uses your phone's recognizer instead — no download, but speech may use the network.

Which tongues work?

40+ through your phone's recognizer. Offline Whisper covers fewer — check settings after install.

What about bank apps and passwords?

The bubble hides in password and bank fields. Mic runs only after you tap. No iPhone app — Android only.
