Vs other voice apps

Our moat is not STT. It is keep-your-keyboard + history/dictionary/snippets + MIT / no account. They transcribe better. We are the product shell.

If you need audio to stay on the phone, pick FUTO, Sayboard, or Whisper IME — not Open Flow. We wrap the system recognizer. That is convenient. It is not a local Whisper stack.
