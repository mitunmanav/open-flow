Speak. It types.
Keep your keyboard.

A free Android app. A small bubble floats over other apps. Tap, talk, tap again. Words go into the field you were already in.

Not a keyboard. English only. MIT. No account. No ads.

Honest. This app does not declare INTERNET and does not upload audio itself. Speech still uses the phone’s system recognizer. On many phones that is Google, and audio can leave the device. Need “voice never leaves this phone”? Use a local Whisper or Vosk app — not this one.

What you get

Bubble

Tap, tap again, or hold. X throws away. Drag to move.

Keep your keyboard

We do not replace Gboard. We sit on top.

History

Search, copy, share. Stays on this phone.

Cleanup + style

Rules on the phone. Not a cloud AI model.

How you use it

  1. Install the APK from Releases.
  2. Turn on Accessibility. Allow the mic.
  3. Open a normal text field (not a password or bank app).
  4. Tap the bubble → speak → tap again.
