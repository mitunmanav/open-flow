Privacy

Last updated 2026-08-13. Contact: GitHub Issues only. Do not send personal data.

  • No account. No ads. No analytics.
  • The APK does not declare INTERNET.
  • History stays on this phone unless you share it.
  • Accessibility is used to find a text field and insert what you just said.
  • Microphone only while you dictate.
  • The phone’s speech engine may still use Google. That is the phone, not an Open Flow server.
  • We hide on many bank apps. Their warning is theirs.
